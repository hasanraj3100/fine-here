About

Hello, it's me, Oasimul Hasan Raj.

I needed somewhere to write things down for myself: random thoughts, stuff I stumbled upon, and the occasional thing I needed to hear. Mostly it's me talking to me. It just happens to be public, and you're welcome to listen in.

The notes are short and usually written in the moment, before I overthink them. Some get a tag: noticed for something I saw, wondered for a question I can't answer yet, wrong for when I change my mind. Most don't bother.

They pile up week by week, and nobody tidies them afterwards, least of all me.
